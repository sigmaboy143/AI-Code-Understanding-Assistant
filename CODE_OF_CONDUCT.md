# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in the CODE.X community a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

We welcome contributions from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Giving constructive feedback.
- Accepting different viewpoints.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community.
- Helping other contributors when possible.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Threats or intimidation.
- Any other behavior that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in an open-source community.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainers through a private GitHub communication channel.

Please provide enough information for the maintainers to understand and investigate the issue.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior, including removing inappropriate content, restricting participation, or taking other necessary measures.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and interactions related to CODE.X.

## License

This Code of Conduct is provided to help maintain a safe and welcoming community around CODE.X.
